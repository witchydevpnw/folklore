Updates

A short record of what we're looking forward to and the reader events we've been part of, newest first.

November 2026

New books by local authors in Little Free Libraries

Brand-new books by local authors have been finding their way into Little Free Libraries around the Tri-Cities. These copies were donated, and they're waiting for whoever opens the little door next.

So far that includes Spellborn by Taylor Rogers and For Our Next Song by Jessica James. If you spot one on your walk, take it home, and pass it along when you're done.

Our Shelf Life program is designed to buy books like these from local, independent bookstores or directly from local authors and put them in free libraries, and we're fundraising for it now. If you'd like to help, you can give on our Support page.

November 2026

Sagebrush and Spines is coming

Sagebrush and Spines is Folklore Society's inaugural spring book fair, and it's the first book fair of its kind in the Tri-Cities. The fair is on May 15, 2027, at the Gesa Carousel of Dreams in Kennewick. It's free to the public, and hours are coming soon.

The day brings together adult and new adult authors, bookish vendors, book clubs, and independent bookstores. The books are adult, new adult, and upper YA for readers 16 and up. A silent auction will be part of the day.

Fundraising at the fair supports the Author Fund, which covers author table fees. That way, author tables at our events can stay free, and your gifts make that possible. Authors and makers who'd like a table can find the interest form at sagebrushandspines.com.

Read about the fair

November 2026

Giving Tuesday is December 1

Giving Tuesday is December 1, and it's a good day to put a gift toward the reading life here in the Tri-Cities. Folklore Society is in its first year, and a gift helps us keep doing the work already in front of us.

The Author Fund covers author table fees. That way, author tables at our events can stay free, and your gifts make that possible. You can give to the Author Fund, or give to the general fund if you'd rather we put the gift where it's needed. Both choices are on our Support page.

Gifts are processed through Zeffy, so we don't see or store your card number. If you have a question before you give, write to hello@folkloresocietywa.org and we'll write back.

See ways to give

November 2026

See you at Booked for the Holidays

We hope to see you on December 5, 2026, at Booked for the Holidays. It's the wandering winter book market hosted by BAGG Books and Obsidian Book Collective, with local authors and bookish vendors set up inside downtown Kennewick shops. The market runs from 11 in the morning until 4 in the afternoon. It's free, and no ticket is needed.

Folklore Society is sponsoring the outdoor beer garden out front of BAGG Books, so come say hello while you wander between the shops. If you'd like to guest bartend with us that day, send a direct message on Instagram at @folkloresocietywa. Details for the market itself are on the Booked for the Holidays site.

Visit the market site

August 20, 2026

A pajama watch party at Vision Venue

On August 20, 2026, readers came in pajamas to Vision Venue in downtown Kennewick for a Practical Magic watch party. Doors were at 7 and the film was at 8. Earth Spirit Mystical Boutique and Obsidian Book Collective presented the night, and Folklore Society poured the drinks. Ages 13 and up were welcome. It was free with a ticket.

It was a cozy night of readers watching a favorite together, and the event page is still up if you want to look back.

Look back at the night

August 15, 2026

Romance readers at BAGG Books

Bookstore Romance Day was August 15, 2026, at BAGG Books in downtown Kennewick. The day ran from 11 in the morning until 6 in the evening. It marked the national Bookstore Romance Day, and it was free and open to everyone.

Carly Joy offered flash tattoos, and Blake Gallows was there for a book signing and a meet and greet. Folklore Society ran the beer garden out back, and we were glad to be part of the day.

Look back at the day

July 4, 2026

The Freedom to Read at BAGG Books

The Freedom to Read was July 4, 2026, at BAGG Books in downtown Kennewick, from 11 in the morning until 4 in the afternoon. Obsidian Book Collective presented the day, and BAGG Books hosted it. It was free and open to everyone.

Readers brought books to swap, looked through banned romance from the shop's shelves, and tried bookish crafts and temporary tattoos. It was one afternoon for a stubborn idea, that you should get to read whatever you want. Take a look at the event page for the full picture.

Look back at the afternoon

June 2026

Our first event, the Harvest Season release party

Folklore Society's first event was the release party for Brynne Weaver's Harvest Season at BAGG Books in downtown Kennewick in June 2026. Readers filled the room around long tables set with black candles, a skull centerpiece, and wrapped cookies.

We poured from a Harvest Season drink menu with two cocktails, Maya's Magical Gimlet and Harper's Moka Martini, and what the bar brought in went back into our programs. It's where Folklore got its start, and we're grateful to everyone who came out that night.
