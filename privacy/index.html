Privacy policy

Privacy

This page explains, in plain language, what information Folklore Society collects through this website, how we use it, and how to reach us about it. This policy is effective November 20, 2026.

Who we are

Folklore Society is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit based in the Tri-Cities, Washington, and our EIN is 42-1823147. You can write to us at hello@folkloresocietywa.org, or by mail at 5405 Warbler Ln, West Richland, WA 99353.

The Stay in touch form

Our Stay in touch form is hosted by Airtable. When you fill it out, we collect your name, your email address, the things you tell us you're interested in, an optional note, and the page you came from. We use that information only to contact you about the things you chose.

Donations

Donations to Folklore Society are processed by Zeffy. Folklore does not see or store your card number at any point.

What we don't do

We do not sell or share your personal information. Right now this website doesn't use any analytics or advertising cookies. If we add Google Analytics or Google Ads conversion tracking in the future, we'll update this page to say so.

Removing your information

If you'd like us to remove your information, email us at hello@folkloresocietywa.org and we'll take care of it. The same address is the best place to send any questions about this policy.

Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top, so you can always see the current version.
