Our next event

Sagebrush and Spines

Sagebrush and Spines is Folklore Society's inaugural spring book fair, and it's the first book fair of its kind in the Tri-Cities. It's free to the public, and we'd love for you to come spend the day with us.

Visit sagebrushandspines.com Stay in touch

The details

Date
May 15, 2027
Hours
Hours coming soon.
Where
Gesa Carousel of Dreams, Kennewick, WA
Cost
Free to the public
Books
Adult, new adult and upper YA (16+)

What to expect

The fair brings together adult and new adult authors, bookish vendors, book clubs and independent bookstores, so you can meet the people writing the books, browse the shelves of local shops, and find out about book clubs near you, all in one day. The vendor tables are for makers whose work is bookish.

The focus is on adult, new adult and upper YA books for readers 16 and up, so this isn't a fair for children's books. A silent auction will be part of the day as well.

Where the money goes

Fundraising at Sagebrush and Spines supports the Author Fund, which covers author table fees. That way, author tables at our events can stay free, and your gifts make that possible.

If you'd like to give before the fair, you can give to Folklore any time through Zeffy.

Give to the Author Fund

Why we're doing this

There are a lot of readers on this side of the state, and we'd like more of the reading life to happen close to home. We hope Sagebrush and Spines is the start of more reader events east of the Cascades, and that it's only the first of many days like it.

Authors and vendors

If you're an author, or a maker whose work is bookish, and you'd like a table at the fair, the details and the author and vendor interest form are on the Sagebrush and Spines website. We'd be glad to have you there.
