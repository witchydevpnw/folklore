2026 is Folklore Society's first year. Here's a plain look at what came in and went out so far this year. A full annual report will follow once the year closes.

Our founders covered startup costs like licenses and permits so Folklore could get going. Gifts and event proceeds go back into our programs, which means the Author Fund, which covers author table fees so author tables at our events can stay free, and Round Table, which buys books for local book clubs reading regional authors.

Folklore Society is a Washington nonprofit corporation recognized as a 501(c)(3), EIN 42-1823147. Questions about our finances are welcome at hello@folkloresocietywa.org.
