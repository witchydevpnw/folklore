About us

A letter from the board

Dear Reader,

We're the ones at the bookstore, at the book club, at the event with the drink in our hand (more on that later). We've been part of this reading community long enough to know exactly what it's capable of.

The big literary events happen hours away in Seattle or Portland, worth the drive sometimes, but not every time, and not for everyone. The Tri-Cities has the readers for something better. Author readings, book fairs, bookish balls, grants for indie authors working outside the big publishing houses. We think this place can become a real home for books in the Pacific Northwest, and we built Folklore to help get it there.

We fundraise to bring authors out to the Tri-Cities, underwrite author visits, and back the people already doing the work here, and when we buy books for events and clubs, we buy them from local, independent bookstores or directly from local authors. The bookstores and reading nights belong to the people running them. Folklore is the part underneath, the fundraising and the money that goes back in. None of us draws a salary from it.

Folklore Society is Washington-incorporated, federally recognized as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and what comes in goes back into our programs.

At some events, we host a pop-up bar, and what it brings in goes back into our programs. It's one of the ways we show up for partners whose events we believe in, and it supports our real work as a nonprofit for Tri-Cities readers and authors.

For the reading life,

Candace, Jessica, Katee & Beth

Board of Directors, Folklore Society

What we do

Bringing writers to readers

We fundraise to bring authors out to the Tri-Cities for events like readings, signings and conversations, and the Author Fund is where that money goes. Right now it supports costs like author table fees, so author tables at our events can stay free, and your gifts make that possible. As the fund grows, we hope it can help with more of what it takes to get an author here.

Championing local

We put our support with the independent bookstores and regional writers who give the Tri-Cities its literary life. When we buy books for events and clubs, we buy them from local, independent bookstores or directly from local authors, and when there's a stage to fill, we look close to home first. You can read how we use funds.

Backing the people already doing the work

The book clubs and reading nights here were going long before Folklore existed, and we'd rather put money behind them than take them over. Through Round Table, we buy books for local book clubs reading regional authors and support the small businesses that host them. We also buy books from independent authors and local bookstores to stock free libraries, so more people can get their hands on local literature and the authors still get paid for their work.

The details

We're readers based in the Tri-Cities, and if you run a bookstore or a reading night here, write books, or just want to know where a gift would go, we'd be glad to hear from you at hello@folkloresocietywa.org. Endpapers, our membership, is on the way.
