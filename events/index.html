How we show up at events

Most of the events on this page belong to the bookstores and collectives who dream them up, and our part is the support underneath, which usually means sponsoring and raising money so the day can happen the way its organizers picture it.

When we host something ourselves, like Sagebrush and Spines, the Author Fund is for covering author table fees. That way, author tables at our events can stay free, and your gifts make that possible.

If you're planning a literary event in the Tri-Cities and think we might be a good partner or sponsor, write to us at hello@folkloresocietywa.org.
