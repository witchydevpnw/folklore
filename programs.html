What we do

Programs

Each of our programs puts money behind a different corner of the Tri-Cities reading life. Here's what they do and how to be part of them.

The programs

Author Fund

Bring writers to readers.

Your gift underwrites author visits to the Tri-Cities: honoraria, travel, lodging, and the small things that make a good author experience into a great one. Gifts of any size add up here, from a warm welcome for a visiting writer to bringing a debut novelist out to read.

Give to the Author Fund

Shelf Life

Put books on shelves.

Every quarter, readers vote on a book by a local or regional author. We buy the winner from a Tri-Cities indie bookstore and place copies in free libraries across the metro. When the next vote opens, we'll make sure you hear about it.

How Shelf Life works

Round Table

Take care of book club night.

When a Tri-Cities book club picks a regional author's book, we buy the copies from a local indie bookstore. How much of this we can do depends on funding, so giving where it's needed most is what keeps the table set for the next club.

How Round Table works

Dedication

Sponsor a bottle.

For $25, we'll label a bottle with your name and a dedication to whoever you're thinking of: a grandmother who read to you, a partner, a friend. It joins the bar lineup at our next event, and afterward the bottle is yours to keep if you want it.
