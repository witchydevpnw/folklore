Programs

Each of our programs puts money behind a different corner of the Tri-Cities reading life. Here's what they do and how to be part of them.

Author Fund

Bring writers to readers.

Your gift underwrites author visits to the Tri-Cities: honoraria, travel, lodging, and the small things that make a good author experience into a great one. Gifts of any size add up here, from a warm welcome for a visiting writer to bringing a debut novelist out to read.

How it works. This is our central fundraising vehicle, and it's what pays for the events that put readers and writers in the same room. Every gift is tied to a real cost, from a $25 welcome for a visiting author to $500 to bring a debut novelist out to read.

Shelf Life Now open · Summer 2026

Put books on shelves.

Every quarter, readers vote on a book by a local or regional author. We buy the winner from a Tri-Cities indie bookstore and place copies in free libraries across the metro.

How it works. Each quarter we draw a shortlist from the submission pool. Readers vote, and every $5 vote is a donation that goes straight to buying the winning book from a local indie bookstore. Each copy gets a Folklore bookplate and lands in a free library around the Tri-Cities.

Who's eligible. Books published in the last five years by an author in Washington, Oregon, or Idaho, orderable through a Tri-Cities indie bookstore, written for young adult or adult readers. Current bookstore partners: BAGG Books, Wildflower Fiction, Ink Drinkers Bookshop, and Adventures Underground.

Round Table Opening fall 2026

Take care of book club night.

When a Tri-Cities book club is reading a regional author's book, we buy the copies from a local indie bookstore of your choice. Vendor-direct, so no money touches the club.

How it works. Tell us about your club and the book you're reading. We confirm eligibility against published criteria, pay the bookstore directly at full retail, and your copies are ready for pickup.

Who's eligible. Local, open, free book clubs reading a Pacific Northwest author's book published in the last five years, for young adult or adult readers, available through a Tri-Cities indie bookstore. In year one we're supporting up to four Round Tables, with up to 12 copies each.
