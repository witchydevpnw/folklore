What's happening✦

Readers came as their coziest selves, with the ones who'd stay up for a little midnight mischief, and settled in for the film. The rest, as any good witch knows, sorted itself out by candlelight.

  • Practical Magic on the big screen: the cult favorite, exactly as the season calls for it
  • Something to sip: drinks crafted by Folklore Society, for the readers who wanted them
  • Pajamas encouraged: come cozy — the comfier the better
  • Popcorn & movie snacks: settle in with the good stuff and a room full of fellow book witches
  • Shop the goodies: Earth Spirit and BAGG Books had merch and treasures to browse and take home

Folklore's part✦

This was a reader event, a room full of people in pajamas watching a film together. Folklore Society poured the drinks, and what came in went back into our general fund, which covers event costs and our programs.

Want a gift to go to a specific fund instead? The Author Fund and the general fund are both still open.
