What's happening✦

There's a certain hour — long after the popcorn's popped and the first margarita's poured — when the ordinary starts to shimmer. Come as your coziest self, bring the ones who'd stir up a little midnight mischief with you, and settle in. The rest, as any good witch knows, tends to sort itself out by candlelight.

  • Practical Magic on the big screen: the cult favorite, exactly as the season calls for it
  • Midnight margaritas & more: cocktails, mocktails, and other beverages crafted by Folklore Society
  • Pajamas encouraged: come cozy — the comfier the better
  • Popcorn & movie snacks: settle in with the good stuff and a room full of fellow book witches
  • Shop the goodies: Earth Spirit and BAGG Books will have merch and treasures to browse and take home

Folklore on the bar✦

The margaritas are ours. Folklore Society is mixing the midnight margaritas and every other pour of the night, and every dollar from the bar goes back into our general fund — the pool that keeps us pouring at events like this one and Round Table, and covers the quiet costs that make them happen.

Want your gift to go to a specific fund instead? Look for the QR code at the event — scan it and you can send your support exactly where you'd like it to land.
