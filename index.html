A reading life,
closer to home.

The Tri-Cities is full of readers, and we're the nonprofit that backs them, from the indie bookstores and local authors to the people who keep book clubs and reading nights going. We raise money so this can be a place with a reading life of its own, where seeing an author on tour doesn't mean a drive over the mountains.

Our work✦

We support local readers and authors in the Tri-Cities. When we buy books for events and clubs, we buy them from local, independent bookstores or directly from local authors, and when there's a stage to fill we look close to home first. The Author Fund covers author table fees. That way, author tables at our events can stay free, and your gifts make that possible. Book clubs and reading series were packing rooms long before Folklore existed, and when we're invited in we put money behind that work and let the people who built it keep leading.

Our next event✦

Sagebrush and Spines

Sagebrush and Spines is our inaugural spring book fair, and it's the first book fair of its kind in the Tri-Cities. Join us on May 15, 2027, at the Gesa Carousel of Dreams in Kennewick, where adult and new adult authors, bookish vendors, book clubs and independent bookstores will all be in one place. It's free to the public, and hours are coming soon.

Before then, you can find us on December 5 at Booked for the Holidays, the wandering winter book market hosted by BAGG Books and Obsidian Book Collective, where Folklore Society is sponsoring the outdoor beer garden out front of BAGG Books. We'd love to see you at both.
