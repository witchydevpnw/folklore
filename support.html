Give

Support Folklore.

Pick a program you love, or give to the general fund and we'll put it wherever the shelves look thinnest that month. If you want the plain version of where gifts and event income go, it is just below.

Choose where to give

Round Table

Take care of book club night.

When a Tri-Cities club lands on a regional author's book, Round Table buys the copies from a local indie so nobody has to choose between reading together and stretching the grocery budget. How many clubs we can meet depends on what comes in, and a gift here is what keeps the next request from waiting in the pool.

How Round Table works

General Fund

Give where it's needed most.

Some of Folklore's work never makes a flyer: licensing, insurance, and the venue deposit that has to clear before an event can be promised in public. A gift to the general fund is how that pile gets tended when a program gift can't stretch that far.

Give where it's needed most (we'll spend it well)

Author Fund

Bring writers to readers.

An author evening in Kennewick or Richland costs real money before anyone opens a book, and the Author Fund is how we plan to keep the table fee off the author's plate. The Author Fund is for covering author table fees. That way, author tables at our events can stay free, and your gifts make that possible.

Give to the Author Fund (and keep an author's table on us)

Thank you for backing the reading life here, and for reading all the way down to the legal part.

Folklore Society is a federally recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN: 42-1823147). Donations are tax-deductible to the extent permitted by law. We process all donations through Zeffy, a platform that charges no fees, so 100% of your gift reaches Folklore.

Questions about giving? Write to hello@folkloresocietywa.org and one of us will write back.

How we use funds

Where gifts and event income go.

Gifts and the income from our events go toward the work we already do in the Tri-Cities.

The Author Fund covers author table fees. That way, author tables at our events can stay free, and your gifts make that possible.

We buy books for events and clubs from local, independent bookstores or directly from local authors.

We also cover event costs like the venue and supplies.

The year-to-date numbers, what came in and what went out, are on our financials page.

Volunteer

Lend a hand.

A lot of what Folklore does happens because people pitch in alongside us. Here are the ways people help right now, and the sign-up below is the easiest way to tell us you're interested.

Help us get ready.

Some of the work happens before an event starts, from setting up tables to letting people around town know it's coming. If you'd like to help with that side of things, tell us on the sign-up form below and we'll be in touch.

Help on the day.
